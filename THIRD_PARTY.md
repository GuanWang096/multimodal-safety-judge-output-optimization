The root Apache-2.0 license covers the authors' original software and its accompanying software documentation. It does not override upstream licenses, grant rights to third-party media, or remove conditions attached to dataset-derived material. Results under `results/` are retained research artifacts; do not interpret the software license as a blanket license for the underlying data.

The model names above identify the evaluated interfaces; exact ModelScope checkpoint revisions remain in the frozen manifests. The excluded InternVL qualification candidate and the separate historical judge-reputation diagnostic are not additional judges in the three-judge confirmatory analysis; consult their original provider terms before obtaining those checkpoints.

Source datasets and model weights must be obtained from their providers. Public cards can change, and access conditions or third-party media rights can apply in addition to a card's license label. Research use is not a blanket exemption from non-commercial or attribution conditions. Dependencies such as Fabric, IPFS and Python/Go packages retain their own licenses and notices. No model weights, original multimedia corpus, production credentials or private node-key material are included in this release.
